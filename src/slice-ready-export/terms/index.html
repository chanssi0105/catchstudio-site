Terms of Use

Version 1.0 · Updated October 7, 2026

1. Service

Slice Ready Export is a Figma plugin operated by catchstudio. It exports selected frames, sections, components and instances as JPG, PNG-24 or PNG-8, split by horizontal guides, slices or direct children. Rendering and file generation run on your device. Platform presets are guidance; check the platform's current requirements yourself.

2. Free trial

The trial offers up to 3 exports within 7 days of the first run, per Figma account. It ends when either limit is reached. An internet connection is required to verify the trial. Each frame exported consumes one credit, regardless of how many slices it contains. Saving a single slice also uses one credit. A multi-frame export requires enough remaining credits for all selected export targets. Analysis and preview do not consume credits.

Credits are authorized before download requests start. If no download request can start, the plugin attempts credit recovery, including retrying on a later run. Once a download request starts, cancelling the system save dialog or stopping remaining files does not automatically restore the credit. Trial limits are account based; reinstalling does not reset them.

3. Purchase and refunds

A $12 one-time purchase through Figma unlocks unlimited exports in all supported formats. Taxes, currency conversion and the final checkout amount are shown by Figma. Payment and refunds follow Figma's Community refund policy and checkout terms.

When Figma reports the account as PAID, exports do not depend on the trial server. This does not guarantee that Figma itself works without a connection or that the plugin can determine purchase status in every offline situation.

4. Saving and checking results

You may save ZIP archives or individual files. Individual saving in the desktop app may open a dialog for each file. JPG supports file-size fitting; reaching a requested limit is not guaranteed for every image. PNG-8 may change colors or transparency. Check cropping, text, colors, resolution, dimensions, file size and platform compatibility before uploading, printing or delivery. You are responsible for rights to the content you export.

5. Changes and availability

Features, presets and compatibility may change as Figma or platforms change. Maintenance, outages or discontinuation may interrupt the service. We will announce material changes through release information or these pages where practicable. A one-time purchase does not promise perpetual compatibility, storage or uninterrupted availability. Nothing here removes rights that applicable law does not allow us to exclude.

6. Data and contact

Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy. Operator and contact: catchstudio representative, catchstudio25@gmail.com, 0507-2097-3010. Contact us about service, purchase or privacy issues.

무료 체험은 피그마 계정당 첫 실행 후 7일 안에 최대 3회입니다. 기간이나 횟수 중 하나가 끝나면 체험이 종료됩니다. 확인에는 인터넷 연결이 필요합니다. 프레임 1개가 1회이며 그 안의 조각 수와 무관합니다. 조각 하나만 저장해도 1회로 계산합니다. 여러 프레임은 내보낼 대상 전체에 필요한 횟수가 남아 있어야 합니다. 분석·미리보기는 차감하지 않습니다.

다운로드 요청 전에 횟수를 승인받습니다. 요청을 하나도 시작하지 못하면 사용 횟수 복구를 시도하며 다음 실행에서 다시 시도할 수 있습니다. 다운로드 요청이 시작된 뒤 시스템 저장 창을 취소하거나 나머지 파일 저장을 중지해도 횟수가 자동 복구되지는 않습니다. 체험은 계정 기준이므로 재설치로 초기화되지 않습니다.
